## 1. Why

`consolidate_memories` takes 23 parameters. Four groups belong together: who wrote it (`Provenance`), the core-memory declaration (`is_core`, `core_reason`, `core_exit_condition`, `core_review_after`, `detail_memory_ids`, which already travel together as `_ConsolidationCoreRequest` internally), two pass-through entity attributes (`metadata`, `memory_type`), and the bulk-only internals (`_in_transaction` and the three `_precomputed_*` values, set only by `_commit_bulk_consolidation_item`). That is 23 - 2 - 4 - 1 - 3 = 13. `scope` stays a flat parameter (it is the visibility of every consolidated memory, not a core input) and `db_connection`/`db_path` stay flat (every one of about 55 test call sites passes `db_connection`; grouping them is the highest-churn option). Rejected: `**kwargs`; a flat mirror dataclass; scope inside the core group; grouping `db_connection`/`db_path`/`_in_transaction` as `Storage`; raising the Sonar limit.
